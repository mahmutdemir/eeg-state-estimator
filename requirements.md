# Requirements

Each requirement has an ID, a statement, and an acceptance criterion with an explicit
numerical tolerance. Every ID must be verified by at least one test marked
`@pytest.mark.req("REQ-0XX")`; `tests/test_traceability.py` fails the suite if any ID
here has no test, and `docs/traceability-matrix.md` is generated from the markers.

Tolerances are stated here rather than in the tests so that the acceptance criterion and
the code that checks it cannot drift apart silently.

**Scope.** This is a demonstration core, not a clinical device. See README for what is
deliberately out of scope.

---

## Contracts

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| REQ-001 | The library shall validate its inputs and fail loudly rather than return a silently wrong result. | A non-finite sample, an empty array, a non-1-D array, a non-positive sample rate, or a taper count exceeding `2·NW − 1` each raise `ValueError` with a message naming the offending argument. |
| REQ-002 | Results shall be deterministic. | The same input produces bitwise-identical output across repeated calls within a process. |

## Spectral estimation

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| REQ-010 | The power spectral density shall be estimated by the multitaper method using DPSS (Slepian) tapers, with time-bandwidth product `NW` and taper count `K` configurable. | `K` defaults to `2·NW − 1`; `K > 2·NW − 1` is rejected (REQ-001). The returned frequency axis is one-sided and spans DC to Nyquist. |
| REQ-011 | For a pure sinusoid, the estimator shall recover the peak frequency. | Recovered peak frequency within **±0.25 Hz** of the true frequency, including frequencies that fall between FFT bins. |
| REQ-012 | For a pure sinusoid of amplitude `A`, the estimator shall recover the signal power. | Power integrated over the analysis bandwidth about the peak within **±5%** of the true `A²/2`. |
| REQ-013 | The power spectral density shall satisfy Parseval's relation. | For broadband input, the integral of the one-sided PSD over frequency equals the time-domain variance within **±2%**. |
| REQ-014 | For white noise, the estimated PSD shall be flat. | Across 1–40 Hz, the PSD deviates from its own band mean by no more than **±10%**. |
| REQ-015 | For `1/f^a` noise, the estimator shall recover the spectral slope. | Slope from a log-log least-squares fit over 1–40 Hz within **±0.15** of the true `a`, for `a` in 0.5 to 2.0. |
| REQ-016 | The library shall report power in named frequency bands. | Band powers for slow-delta (0.5–4 Hz), theta (4–8 Hz), alpha (8–12 Hz) and beta (12–30 Hz); band powers over a partition of a range sum to the total power over that range within **±1%**. |
| REQ-017 | The library shall report the spectral edge frequency. | SEF at a configurable fraction (default 0.95) of total power; monotonically non-decreasing in the fraction. |
| REQ-018 | Spectral estimation shall be scale-equivariant. | Scaling the input by `c` scales the PSD by `c²` within floating-point tolerance. |
| REQ-019 | Spectral estimation shall be independent of the sample rate used to represent the same signal. | The same continuous-time sinusoid sampled at different rates yields peak frequency agreeing within REQ-011's tolerance and integrated peak power within REQ-012's tolerance. |

## Recursive state estimation

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| REQ-020 | The library shall provide a scalar Kalman filter with a random-walk process model that reports a posterior variance with every state estimate. | Every update returns both the posterior mean and its variance; the variance is strictly positive. |
| REQ-021 | The filter shall expose the innovation and the innovation variance at every step. | Both are returned by every update. They are the basis of REQ-024 and REQ-025 and must be observable without access to the internal state. |
| REQ-022 | The filter shall recover a known latent trajectory from data generated by its own model. | On self-simulated data, RMSE against the true state is at least 2× lower than the RMSE of the raw observations. |
| REQ-023 | The reported credible intervals shall be calibrated. | Across ≥200 independent simulated runs, nominal 95% intervals contain the true state **95% ± 3%** of the time. |
| REQ-024 | Filter innovations shall be white under a correctly specified model. | A Ljung–Box test on the normalized innovations does not reject at **α = 0.05**. |
| REQ-025 | The filter shall be consistent in the normalized-estimation-error sense. | Average NEES over ≥200 runs falls within the two-sided 95% chi-square bound for its degrees of freedom. |
| REQ-026 | The filter shall converge from a deliberately poor initialization. | Started with a state estimate far from the truth, the estimate reaches and stays within 3 posterior standard deviations of the true state within a stated number of steps, and the posterior variance converges to the Riccati fixed point independently of its initial value. |

## Verification infrastructure

| ID | Requirement | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|
| REQ-030 | Every requirement in this document shall be verified by at least one test. | `tests/test_traceability.py` parses the IDs from this file and fails if any has no test marked with it. |
